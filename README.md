## Data Visualization Principles

My experience with business intelligence reporting has involved applying several important visualization principles:

- **Clarity:** Presenting information in a format that is easy to interpret.
- **Accuracy:** Ensuring calculations and visual representations reflect the underlying data.
- **Relevance:** Selecting metrics that support business objectives.
- **Interactivity:** Allowing users to explore information through filters and dashboard controls.
- **Data Storytelling:** Communicating analytical findings to support informed decisions.

Additional examples of statistical visualizations and specialized chart types will be included as supporting evidence where applicable.
